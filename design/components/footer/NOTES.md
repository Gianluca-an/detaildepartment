# Footer 9 — Inspiration catalog + Direction B footer

Two Dribbble shots the client flagged as "potential footer ideas." Cataloged as
reference only — we did not pixel-copy them. The Direction-B footer below is an
original build in our own system (charcoal + bronze, Sora/Inter).

## Reference shots

### 1. "Modern Automotive Brand Identity | Car Detailing" — ZayaanInk
- Shot: https://dribbble.com/shots/27701451
- Brand in the shot: "RINZO", tagline "The Standard of Clean".
- Read: dark, high-contrast identity; custom geometric wordmark; circular accent
  mark; a single warm energy accent (yellow) over near-monochrome.
- Dribbble palette chips: `#030202 #F6F6F6 #5B3A2F #D6AA02 #A5563A #C7A698 #857D82`
  (near-black ground, off-white ink, warm browns, one gold pop).
- What we borrow: the big confident wordmark + one warm accent over dark
  monochrome. We swap the yellow pop for our bronze (#B8935A) and keep the
  brown mids that already rhyme with our system.

### 2. "Car Wash and Car Detailing" — Amish Riaz
- Shot: https://dribbble.com/shots/24674170
- Read: a car wash / detailing landing UI (product-marketing footer layout —
  service columns, contact block, CTA band).
- What we borrow: the multi-column service/contact structure and a full-width
  CTA band sitting above the legal row.

## Direction B footer — what's in it
- Oversized "DETAIL DEPARTMENT" wordmark (edge-fade), bronze "/" slash motif.
- CTA band: "Marcar avaliação" (interactive-hover-button styling, header 4).
- Three services (our locked set): Detalhe Automóvel · Proteção Cerâmica ·
  Restauro (faróis, chapa e pintura).
- Contact: WhatsApp click-to-chat, telefone, morada (Grande Lisboa).
- Horário + redes sociais (Instagram).
- PT compliance row: NIF, Livro de Reclamações eletrónico, Política de
  Privacidade / Cookies (RGPD). All placeholders — fill with real data at build.
- Bronze hairlines, near-sharp 2px corners, prefers-reduced-motion respected.

Placement is NOT fixed — this is exploratory (UI-sniping). Numbered "9" per the
client's ascending scheme; it can live at the true page foot or fold earlier.
